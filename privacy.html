Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Studio homeTerms of Service

This policy explains how Quiet Place Studio handles information when you use the website. The current Studio is a static website and publication tracker. It does not currently offer TikTok sign-in or an active TikTok API connection.

1. Information you enter

The publication tracker lets you enter video URLs, publication status, and performance figures such as views, likes, comments, and shares. In the current version, this tracker data is saved in your browser's local storage on the device and browser you use. It is not sent to a Quiet Place Studio application server by the tracker.

2. Local storage

Because tracker information is stored locally, it may not appear on another device or browser. Clearing browser data, using private browsing, or changing devices may remove or make this information unavailable. You can avoid entering information you do not want stored in that browser.

3. Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP address, browser information, and request logs as part of delivering and securing its hosting service. That processing is governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement.

4. TikTok connection

TikTok account authorization and automatic retrieval of account or video analytics are not enabled in the current website. If an official TikTok connection is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that feature is made available to explain the exact information requested, purposes, retention, and deletion controls. Do not treat this page as notice that TikTok data is currently being accessed.

5. Third-party links

The site may link to TikTok, Meta, GitHub, or other third-party services. When you open those services, they may collect information under their own privacy policies. Quiet Place Studio does not control their practices.

6. Children

The site is intended for people who are old enough to use the connected social-media services under those services' rules. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

7. Security and retention

Tracker data remains in the local browser storage until it is overwritten or cleared by the user. No password, TikTok login credential, or payment-card information is requested by the current Studio website. Do not share API keys, client secrets, access tokens, or passwords in public fields, screenshots, or issue reports.

8. Your choices and contact

You can stop using the site and clear its locally stored browser data through your browser settings. For questions or privacy requests, contact the project maintainer through the Quiet Place Studio GitHub project. Do not include sensitive account credentials in a report.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
